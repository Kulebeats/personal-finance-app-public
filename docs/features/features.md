# Features Overview

## Accounts and Transactions

- Manual account creation and management
- Transaction create/edit with merchant selection
- Filters for merchants, accounts, tags, goals, amount, and status

## Budgets and Categories

- Category groups and rollups
- Budget summary views with income and expense sections
- Category management and budget defaults

## Merchants

- Merchant CRUD with notes, category, default category
- Logo uploads and alias management
- Duplicate detection and merge workflows
- Merchant analytics and report exports

## Rules Engine

- Conditions: merchant, amount, description, category, type
- Actions: categorize, rename, set merchant/category, hide

## Reports and Dashboards

- Annual dashboard, cash flow, spending, income, net worth
- Merchant spending charts and filters
- Widgets for budgets, transactions, top merchants

## Receipts and Attachments

- Receipt uploads and OCR parsing
- Transaction attachments

## Investments and Goals

- Holdings and performance views
- Goal projections and reminders

## Settings

- Profile, display, notifications, security
- Household preferences and member management
