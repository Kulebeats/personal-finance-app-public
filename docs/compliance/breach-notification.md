# Breach Notification Process

## Purpose

Define the process for notifying regulators and users when a security incident involves personal data.

## Scope

- All incidents involving personal data or financial records
- Applicable jurisdictions (Zambia DPA, GDPR if applicable)

## Triggers

- Confirmed exposure of personal data
- Unauthorized access to financial records
- Loss of data confidentiality or integrity

## Timeline Targets

- Regulator notification target: within 24 hours for Zambia DPA regulated incidents
- User notification target: as soon as practicable after containment
- Internal notification: within 1 hour of confirmation

## Notification Steps

1. Confirm incident scope and affected data categories
2. Draft regulator notice with required details
3. Draft user notice with clear actions
4. Obtain approval from Compliance Lead and Incident Commander
5. Deliver notifications and record evidence

## Required Notice Content

- Incident summary and timeline
- Data categories affected
- Estimated number of affected users
- Mitigation steps taken
- Contact point for follow-up

## Evidence and Recordkeeping

- Store copies of all notifications
- Record delivery timestamps
- Attach evidence to incident record

## Templates

### Regulator Notice

Subject: Data Breach Notification - [Incident ID]

Summary: [High-level description]
Discovery date: [Date and time]
Containment date: [Date and time]
Data categories affected: [List]
Estimated affected users: [Count]
Mitigation actions: [Actions taken]
Contact: privacy@yourdomain.example

### User Notice

Subject: Security Notice - [Short Description]

Hello [User],

We are writing to inform you of a security incident that may affect your account.

What happened: [Description]
When: [Date and time]
What data was involved: [Specific categories]
What we have done: [Actions taken]
What you should do: [User actions]

If you have questions, contact privacy@yourdomain.example.

## Review Cadence

- Review annually and after any P1 or P2 incident
