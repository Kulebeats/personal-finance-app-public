  runtime/bootstrap pins, with bounded output/process cleanup. Permit addons only

  are trusted components. Node permission flags/JS wrappers cannot confine hostile
  addons. Use only reviewed buffer inputs; do not claim generic native network or
  filesystem isolation, resistance to malicious same-user processes, or full app
