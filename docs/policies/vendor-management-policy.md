# Vendor Management Policy

## Purpose

Manage risk from third-party services, vendors, and dependencies.

## Scope

- Cloud providers and hosting
- Email and notification services
- Analytics, OCR, or AI providers
- Open source dependencies

## Vendor Inventory

- Maintain a centralized inventory of all vendors
- Record data types shared, access level, and contract owner
- Classify vendors by criticality and data sensitivity

## Due Diligence

- Security questionnaire or assessment for critical vendors
- Review SOC 2 or ISO reports if available
- Verify data protection terms and breach notification clauses

## Risk Classification

| Level | Criteria | Review Cadence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Processes PII or financial data | Annual |
| High | Supports core availability | Annual |
| Medium | Limited data access | Biennial |
| Low | No sensitive data | As needed |

## Contract Requirements

- Data processing agreement for PII access
- Defined SLA and uptime targets
- Breach notification timelines
- Data retention and deletion commitments

## Ongoing Monitoring

- Track vendor incidents and public advisories
- Reassess vendors after major incidents
- Document any exceptions or compensating controls

## Offboarding

- Revoke access and rotate credentials
- Confirm data deletion from vendor systems
- Update vendor inventory and risk register

## Review Cadence

- Annual review of vendor inventory
- Quarterly review of critical vendor risk
